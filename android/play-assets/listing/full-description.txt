Ten minutes turns into fifteen. Twenty turns into thirty-five. If your guesses run short, a plan built on them is late before it starts. Shoes On plans with how long things really take you.

SET THE TIME YOU LEAVE
Pick when you need to walk out the door and the steps before it: shower, get dressed, breakfast, pack your bag, shoes and keys. Shoes On works backward from the door and tells you when to start.

TAILORED TO YOU
Tell it how far your guesses usually run over. If getting ready feels like an hour but really takes an hour and a half, your plan starts earlier from day one. Then, as you tap Done on each step, it learns your real time for every step, your overall pace, and how long you usually take to get going after the alert.

KNOW WHEN YOU'RE SLIPPING
While you get ready, one screen shows the step you're on, the time left for it, and whether you're on track or minutes behind. The screen stays on while you get ready, and the step you're on, with a countdown and a Done button, sits in your notifications.

ALERTS ALONG THE WAY
You get an alert to start getting ready, a heads-up before a long step runs out, a nudge when a step's time is up, a warning 10 minutes before you leave, and one when it's time to go. Allow alarms and reminders and they arrive on the minute.

BUILT FOR MORNINGS THAT GO SIDEWAYS
Missed the get-ready alert? Two follow-ups tell you when starting right now still gets you out the door. If you fall behind mid-routine, it offers to drop one later step that wins the time back. On an unusual day, skip the routine or give it a one-off leave time without touching your usual plan, right from the alert or the home screen. Done and Skip can be undone for a few seconds.

FREE, AND STAYING FREE
Your first routine, with every alert, the step-by-step coach and all the learning.

SHOES ON PRO
Every routine: workdays, school runs, the gym, weekends. Each learns its own times and keeps its own days and alerts.
