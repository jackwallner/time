# Privacy Policy: Shoes On

> How Shoes On handles your data.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/time/privacy-policy.html

Effective October 20, 2026

## Privacy Policy

Shoes On keeps your routines and timings on your device: your iPhone and paired Apple Watch, or your Android phone. There is no account, nothing is sold, and there are no advertising trackers.

## What the app stores

Your routines, their steps and guesses, your answer to the pace question, and the times you tap Done or leave. These are stored in the app's container on your device and sent to your own Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity. They are not uploaded to any server.

## Notifications

With your permission, the app schedules local notifications on your device. They are created and delivered on the device; no push server is involved.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm your purchase. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, purchase records, and simple counts of how many times a purchase screen was shown. It does not receive your routines or timings. RevenueCat's privacy policy is at [revenuecat.com/privacy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy).

## On Android

The Android app stores the same things in its own private app storage on your phone and leaves them out of device backups and transfers. Alerts are scheduled on the phone with Android's alarm service. Purchases are processed by Google Play and confirmed through RevenueCat, which receives the same anonymous identifier, purchase records and purchase screen counts described above, and nothing about your routines or timings. After a few on-time mornings, Google Play may show its own rating card; Shoes On never sees what you write there.

## Deleting your data

Settings has a button to forget your timings. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
