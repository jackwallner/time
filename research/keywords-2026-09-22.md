## Decisions

- Name and subtitle keep the niche (time blindness, leave on time, ADHD
  routines): they are the positioning and ownable at difficulty 5-15, and
  autocomplete confirms people type them, but they carry no volume.
- Keyword fields spend on the heads that combine with the name and subtitle:
  routine, timer, reminder, countdown, planner, morning, and the local word
  for ADHD where the subtitle lacks it. Source of truth:
  `scripts/keyword_research.py`, built by `scripts/build-locale-metadata.py`.
- US targets tagged in Astro: morning routine, visual timer, visual schedule,
  neurodivergent (`target`); adhd, adhd planner (`wall`).
- False friends rejected: `on time` (transit apps), `commute` (transit),
  `get ready` (games), `countdown` as a target (event-countdown SERP; kept only
  as a combining word).
