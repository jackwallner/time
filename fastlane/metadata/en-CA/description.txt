Ten minutes turns into fifteen. Twenty turns into thirty-five. If your guesses run short, a plan built on them is late before it starts. Shoes On plans with how long things really take you.

SET THE TIME YOU LEAVE
Pick when you need to walk out the door and the steps before it: shower, get dressed, breakfast, pack your bag, shoes and keys. Shoes On works backward from the door and tells you when to start.

TAILORED TO YOU
Tell it how far your guesses usually run over. If getting ready feels like an hour but really takes an hour and a half, your plan starts earlier from day one. Then, as you tap Done on each step, it learns your real time for every step, your overall pace, and how long you usually take to get going after the alert.

KNOW WHEN YOU'RE SLIPPING
While you get ready, one screen shows the step you're on, the time left for it, and whether you're on track or minutes behind. The same view sits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a Done button.

ALERTS THAT MATTER
Time to get ready. Wrap up this step. Still in the shower? Leave in 10 minutes. Time to go. Alerts are Time Sensitive, so they can come through a Focus if you allow it.

FREE, AND STAYING FREE
Your first routine, with every alert, the Lock Screen view and all the learning.

SHOES ON PRO
Every routine (workdays, school runs, the gym, weekends) and the Apple Watch coach, which shows your step and pace on your wrist and lets you tap Done from there.
