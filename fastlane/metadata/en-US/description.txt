Shoes On is a leave-on-time planner for time blindness. Set when you need to walk out the door, and it works backward through your routine to tell you when to start, using how long each step really takes you. Your first routine is free with every alert, and there is no account.

SET THE TIME YOU LEAVE
• Pick your leave time and the steps before it, such as shower, get dressed, breakfast, pack your bag, and shoes and keys
• Shoes On works backward from the door and tells you when to start

LEARNS YOUR REAL TIMES
• Tell it how far your guesses usually run over, and your plan starts earlier from day one
• Each time you tap Done, it learns your real time for that step and your overall pace
• It learns how long you take to get going after the alert, and moves the alert earlier to match
• Your original guess stays visible next to your real time

WHILE YOU GET READY
• One screen shows the step you are on, the time left for it, and whether you are on track or minutes behind
• The same view sits on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with a Done button
• Done and Skip can be undone for a few seconds

ALERTS
• An alert to start getting ready, with two follow-ups if you miss it that say when starting now still gets you out
• A heads-up 2 minutes before a long step runs out, and a nudge when a step's time is up
• A warning 10 minutes before you leave, and one when it is time to go
• Alerts are Time Sensitive, so they can come through a Focus if you allow it

WHEN THE MORNING CHANGES
• If you fall behind, it offers to drop one later step that wins the time back
• Skip a day, or give it a one-off leave time, without touching your usual plan

WIDGETS AND CONTROLS
• A Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widget counts down to your start time
• A Start Getting Ready control for Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button

PRIVACY
• No account, no ads and no cross-app tracking
• Your routines and timings stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch

FREE AND SHOES ON PRO
Your first routine is free with everything above, and it does not expire. Shoes On Pro is an optional monthly or yearly subscription that adds:
• Every routine you need, such as workdays, school runs, the gym and weekends
• The Apple Watch coach, which shows your step and pace on your wrist and lets you tap Done from there
• The watch face complication

New subscribers get a free trial, and the app shows its length before you start. Prices appear in the app before you buy and vary by region. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Shoes On is a planning aid. It does not diagnose or treat ADHD or any other condition.
